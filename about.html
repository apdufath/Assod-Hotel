Hotel Story

A modern landmark of hospitality in Hargeisa

Assod Hotel stands on Principal Road as a contemporary destination for travelers who value comfort, warmth, and reliable service.

From leisure guests exploring Hargeisa to business visitors hosting meetings, we welcome every stay with genuine Somali hospitality and polished modern amenities.
